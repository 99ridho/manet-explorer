## Found and fixed during the pass

- Network nodes rendered at about 9px in the fixed-height card; the canvas scale went from 80 to 60 units per slide unit.
- Links jumped while nodes animated after Randomize; links now share the nodes' spring.
- The RREP dot ended on top of the receiving node's label; packet dots now stop at 75 % of the link.
- The Find bridges live fields read `?` until the run ended; they now count as the search finds bridges.
- Changing operation kept the previous run's steps, so the new listing highlighted a line from the old one (a dsa-course behavior); the shell now clears the steps.
- The home card said "5 operations" for reactive routing, although a student sees three per protocol; cards now list the operation names.
- "C now forwards to D through D": the last RREP hop now reads "C now forwards straight to D."
